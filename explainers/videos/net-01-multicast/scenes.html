What bites in production
THREE FAILURE MODES, ALL SILENT
Wrong interface
The join leaves by the default route, not the feed NIC. Name the interface.
No querier
Nobody refreshes membership; snooping can age the port out. The feed just stops.
Small buffers
A burst at the open overflows the socket buffer; the packets are gone.
One send to many.
IGMP is the contract that keeps it flowing.
