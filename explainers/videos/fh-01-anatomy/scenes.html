What breaks a feed handler
Slow work on the book thread
A log write, a page fault, a lock. Packets queue behind it and every book goes stale together.
A consumer that falls behind
Packet ring full: the rx thread drops the packet and counts it. Recovered like any other loss.
Trusting a book after a gap
One missing delete leaves a phantom order on the book until the day ends.
