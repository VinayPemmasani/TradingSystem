# big-04-cboe: Cboe

## Key facts and sources
- Cboe opened on 26 April 1973 as "the world's first exchange for listed options trading". SPX Weeklys were added in 2005, and by 2023 SPX had expirations on every weekday. Source: https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/the-creation-of-listed-options-at-cboe
- From the Cboe 2025 Annual Report / 10-K (https://s202.q4cdn.com/174824971/files/doc_downloads/2026/03/2025-Annual-Report.pdf):
  - VIX futures launched in 2004 and VIX options in 2006; the Bats merger closed on 28 Feb 2017.
  - 2025 US options: 4.6 bn contracts across 4 exchanges, ADV 18.4 M (a record).
  - SPX ADV was a record 3.9 M, with 0DTE at 59%; European equities share was 25%.
  - Cboe Titanium is the unified in-house platform.
  - OCC is the sole clearing provider for Cboe's US options exchanges.
  - Cboe Clear Europe averaged €69 bn of cleared value a day in 2025, with 73% netted, leaving €19 bn to settle.
  - Cboe holds exclusive US rights to list S&P 500 index options through 31 Dec 2032.
- From the Q4/FY2025 results (https://ir.cboe.com/news/news-details/2026/Cboe-Global-Markets-Reports-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-2025-and-Full-Year/default.aspx):
  - Net revenue $2,429.1 M, up 17%.
  - Gross revenue: derivatives $2,243.9 M, cash and spot $1,834.8 M, Data Vantage $635.5 M.
  - Q4 net revenue by segment: Options $433.1 M out of about $671 M in total, which is roughly two thirds.
  - Q4 market share: total options 29.2%, US equities 9.4%.

- VIX "takes as input the market prices of SPX options and SPXW options": https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/Volatility_Index_Methodology_Cboe_Volatility_Index.pdf

## Unsure / left out

- "OCC is shared by all US options exchanges" rests on OCC's role as the clearing house for US listed options. Cboe's report confirms it only for Cboe's own exchanges.
- The number of US options exchanges is left out because I did not verify it.
