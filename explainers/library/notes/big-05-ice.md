# big-05-ice: ICE

## Key facts and sources
- ICE launched in Atlanta on 10 May 2000 as an online energy market. It bought the IPE (home of Brent) in 2001, listed in 2005, bought NYBOT in 2007 and took over NYSE in 2013. Sources: https://www.ice.com/insights/conversations/inside-the-ice-house/ice-founder-and-ceo-jeff-sprecher-on-20-years-at-the-helm-of-intercontinental-exchange and the ICE 10-K filings (for example https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1571949/000157194916000020/ice2015123110k.htm, which covers the 2015 IDC deal for about $5.2 bn).
- ICE describes Brent as "the global barometer for oil pricing" and TTF as the European hub with the greatest number of market participants: https://ice.com/publicdocs/Exchanges-Natural-Gas-Whitepaper_202407.pdf
- 2025 statistics: 2.4 bn futures and options contracts (record), ADV 9.3 M, Brent ADV 1.5 M, TTF ADV up 22%. Open interest hit a record 113.4 M on 11 Dec 2025, and Brent open interest was about 6 M at year end. Source: https://ir.theice.com/press/news-details/2026/Intercontinental-Exchange-Reports-December-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Statistics
- FIA's 2025 ranking puts ICE 5th with 5.0 bn contracts. FIA's count includes NYSE options. Source: https://www.fia.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/Q4%202025%20ETD%20webinar.pdf
- iMpact multicast feed: top-5 aggregated price levels, snapshots for recovery, and order entry over FIX or SBE. Source: https://www.ice.com/connectivity/multicast-price-feed
- FY2025 net revenue was $9.9 bn, split as follows (source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1571949/000110465926010646/tm264989d1_ex99-1.htm):
  - Exchanges $5.4 bn, of which energy $2.2 bn
  - Fixed Income and Data Services $2.4 bn
  - Mortgage Technology $2.1 bn
  - recurring revenue about $5.1 bn, roughly half

## Unsure / left out
- ICE's Brent market share against CME's WTI is not stated, because no primary source gives a like-for-like figure.
- "Can only close on ICE" means Brent futures listed on ICE Futures Europe and cleared at ICE Clear Europe. Look-alike contracts elsewhere are separate contracts.
- The FIA count includes NYSE equity options, so the narration says so.
