# ac-06-credit: Corporate bonds and RFQ trading

Key facts and sources
- US corporate bond ADV $66.9 bn (2026 through October), $12.1 tn outstanding (Q2 2026): SIFMA US Corporate Bonds Statistics, https://www.sifma.org/research/statistics/us-corporate-bonds-statistics
- About 28,000 public (and 5,000 Rule 144A) TRACE-eligible bond issues on a median day, 2014 data: SEC DERA white paper "Pre-trade Information in the Corporate Bond Market" (Craig, Kim, Woo, Oct 2020), https://www.sec.gov/files/corporate_bond_white_paper.pdf ; RFQ responses go only to the requester.
- TRACE: report within 15 minutes of execution (Rule 6730), disseminated immediately; IG trades above $5 m shown as "5MM+", HY above $1 m as "1MM+": https://www.finra.org/filing-reporting/trade-reporting-and-compliance-engine-trace/trace-reporting-timeframes
- FINRA dropped its proposal to cut the 15-minute limit to one minute: Regulatory Notice 25-17, https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/25-17
- T+1 covers corporate debt: investor.gov T+1 bulletin.
