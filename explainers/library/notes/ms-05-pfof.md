# ms-05-pfof: Payment for order flow and wholesalers

## Key facts and sources
- SEC (Dec 14, 2022, order competition proposal): brokers route "more than 90%" of individual investors' marketable orders in NMS stocks to six wholesalers; qualified auctions for segmented orders (accounts averaging < 40 trades/day over six months), exception for midpoint-or-better fills; "competitive shortfall" ≈ $1.5bn/yr. https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2022-225 ; https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-order-competition-20221214
- Withdrawn June 12, 2025 (with Reg Best Execution): https://www.sec.gov/rules/2022/12/regulation-best-execution ; https://www.proskauer.com/alert/sec-withdraws-fourteen-rule-proposals
- Rule 605 amendments adopted Mar 6, 2024. https://www.sec.gov/files/34-99679-fact-sheet.pdf
- Robinhood Financial Rule 606, Q4 2025, October 2025, S&P 500 stocks: Virtu 46.32% / 73.11¢ per 100 sh; G1 14.62% / 83.15; Jane Street 13.47% / 101.25; Citadel 10.30% / 42.80; HRT 9.28% / 66.75; Two Sigma 6.01% / 75.42 (market orders). https://cdn.finra.org/606/2025/165998_606_NMS_2025_Q4_V1.pdf
