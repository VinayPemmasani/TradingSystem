# big-08-jpx: Japan Exchange Group

## Key facts and sources
- TSE founded on 15 May 1878; trading floor closed in 1999. Source: https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/corporate/jpx-profile/tse/04-01.html
- JPX history (source: https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/corporate/about-jpx/history/index.html):
  - JPX established on 1 Jan 2013.
  - On 16 Jul 2013, OSE's cash market merged into TSE and OSE's derivatives clearing moved into JSCC.
  - On 24 Mar 2014, TSE's derivatives moved to Osaka Exchange.
  - TOCOM became a subsidiary on 1 Oct 2019.
- arrowhead (source: https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/systems/equities-trading/01.html):
  - Launched 4 Jan 2010; arrowhead4.0 since 5 Nov 2024.
  - Order response about 0.2 ms; information dissemination about 0.5 ms.
  - Itayose at the open and close, zaraba in between; 5-minute pre-closing, then itayose at 15:30.
  - FLEX Market by Order distributes every quote by order.
  - The 30-minute extension: https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/corporate/news/news-releases/1030/20241103-01.html
- FY ended March 2026 (source: https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/corporate/news/news-releases/0063/vk0khi000001cvf0-att/Q4FY2025_E_0428.pdf):
  - Operating revenue ¥198.7 bn: trading ¥77.4 bn, clearing ¥54.2 bn, information ¥33.7 bn, listing ¥18.7 bn, system-related ¥13.8 bn.
  - TSE ADV ¥7.52 tn.
  - Nikkei 225 futures ADV 137,175 (large-contract equivalent).
  - 10-year JGB futures ADV 45,286.
