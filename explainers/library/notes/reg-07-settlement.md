# reg-07-settlement: Settlement and T+1

## Key facts and sources
- **US T+1:** adopted Feb 15, 2023 (press release 2023-29, Release 34-96930); compliance May 28, 2024; T+3 set in 1993 (replacing five business days), T+2 in 2017; "time is money and time is risk" (Chair Gensler). https://sec.gov/news/press-release/2023-29 ; https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2024-62
- **Same-day processing:** broker-dealers need agreements or policies to complete allocations, confirmations and affirmations as soon as technologically practicable and no later than end of trade date (2023-29). Rule numbers 15c6-1 / 15c6-2 from the SEC small entity compliance guide title: https://www.sec.gov/investment/settlement-cycle-small-entity-compliance-guide-15c6-1-15c6-2-204-2
- **EU:** Regulation (EU) 2025/2075 amending CSDR, OJ Oct 14, 2025, applies from Oct 11, 2027: https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2025/10/published-in-oj-regulation-eu-2025-2075-amending-csdr-as-regards-a-shorter-settlement-cycle-in-the-eu/
- **Worked example:** Thu Jul 3, 2025 trade; Fri Jul 4 US market holiday; T+1 = Mon Jul 7; T+2 would be Tue Jul 8.
