# reg-09-surveillance: Spoofing, layering and surveillance

## Key facts and sources
- **Statute:** 7 U.S.C. 6c(a)(5)(C) (CEA 4c(a)(5)(C)): "spoofing (bidding or offering with the intent to cancel the bid or offer before execution)". https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/7/6c ; added by Dodd-Frank (2010) per DOJ: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndil/pr/high-frequency-trader-convicted-disrupting-commodity-futures-market-first-federal
- **Coscia:** convicted Nov 3, 2015 on 6 counts of commodities fraud and 6 of spoofing; first federal prosecution under the provision; two algorithms; nearly $1.4 million in less than three months in 2011; CME and ICE Futures Europe; sentenced to 3 years (release Jul 13, 2016). https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndil/pr/high-frequency-trader-sentenced-three-years-prison-disrupting-futures-market-first
- **JPMorgan:** CFTC release 8260-20, Sep 29, 2020: $920.2 million (restitution $311.7M, disgorgement $172.0M, penalty $436.4M), precious metals and Treasury futures, at least 2008-2016, hundreds of thousands of orders; largest CFTC monetary relief. https://cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8260-20
- **RTS 6 Art. 13:** firm-level market abuse surveillance; alerts by start of next trading day. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/589/data.html
- **Lab:** ITCH A/X/D/E carry order reference numbers (docs/specs/itch50-layout.md); drop copy carries the ITCH order ref at offset 49.
