# big-10-why: Why liquidity concentrates

## Key facts and sources
- Pagano, M. (1989), "Trading Volume and Asset Liquidity", QJE 104(2):255–274. When traders choose where to trade, trading concentrates, and the model has multiple equilibria.
- Admati, A. & Pfleiderer, P. (1988), "A Theory of Intraday Patterns: Volume and Price Variability", RFS 1(1):3–40. Discretionary traders cluster at the same times.
- FIA counted 119.3 bn futures and options contracts in 2025, with NSE 34.7 bn, BSE 24.4 bn and B3 11.1 bn (59% between them). Sources: https://www.fia.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/Q4%202025%20ETD%20webinar.pdf and https://www.fia.org/fia/articles/etd-volume-december-2025
- WFE FY2025: NSE had 88.9% of global stock index options and CME 60.8% of interest rate options. Source: https://www.world-exchanges.org/our-work/articles/fy-2025-market-highlights
- SwapClear "over 90%" of IRS notional cleared globally: LSEG Annual Report 2025.
- Nasdaq 2025 matched share: US equities 14.2%, Nordic 72.2% (Nasdaq 2025 10-K). Cboe US equities 9.4% in Q4 2025 (Cboe Q4 2025 results).
- Cboe's exclusive S&P 500 options rights run through 2032 (Cboe 2025 Annual Report).
- The NYSE closing-auction record of over $205 bn in 2025 comes from the ICE 2025 records release.
