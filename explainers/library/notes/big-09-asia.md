# big-09-asia: HKEX, Shanghai and Shenzhen

## Key facts and sources
- HKEX milestones: Hong Kong exchanges unified in 1980; HKEX formed and listed in 2000; LME bought in 2012; Shanghai Connect on 17 Nov 2014; Shenzhen Connect on 5 Dec 2016. Source: https://sc.hkex.com.hk/gb/www.hkexgroup.com/About-HKEX/About-HKEX/HKEX-Milestones?sc_lang=en
- HKEX 2025 results (source: https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/News/News-Release/2026/260226news/2025-Q4-Results-Presentation_E.pdf):
  - Revenue HK$29.2 bn (+30%), split cash 50%, derivatives 24%, commodities 11%, data & connectivity 8%.
  - Headline ADT HK$249.8 bn (+90%).
  - 119 IPOs raising HK$286.9 bn, ranked no. 1 globally.
  - Northbound ADT RMB 212.4 bn.
- Stock Connect mechanics (source: https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Mutual-Market/Stock-Connect/Getting-Started/Information-Booklet-and-FAQ/Investor_Book_En.pdf):
  - An SEHK subsidiary routes orders to SSE/SZSE.
  - HKSCC is a clearing participant of ChinaClear and settles on a net basis.
  - Daily quotas are RMB 52 bn northbound and RMB 42 bn southbound, for each market.
- SSE was founded on 26 Nov 1990: https://english.sse.com.cn/aboutsse/overview/. At end 2025 it had 2,299 listed companies and a market cap of RMB 64.69 tn: https://english.sse.com.cn/news/publications/newsletter/c/10804613/files/3009d96b0f3048ceac3e47569bd025dc.html
- SZSE had 2,884 listed companies on 26 Dec 2025: https://www.szse.cn/enydyl/new/P020251229541824095032.pdf
- WFE reports 2025 value-traded growth of +65.8% for Shenzhen and +60.9% for Shanghai: https://www.world-exchanges.org/our-work/articles/fy-2025-market-highlights

## Unsure / left out
- SZSE's founding date was not verified, so the timeline leaves out Shenzhen's founding.
- HKEX's matching-engine details were not covered by a primary source read, so the tech scene covers only Stock Connect routing and clearing.
- "Huge home investor base" is qualitative.
- Quotas are as stated in the investor book read; the current values may be revised.
