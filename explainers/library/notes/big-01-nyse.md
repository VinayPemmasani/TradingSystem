# big-01-nyse: NYSE

## Key facts and sources
- History: Buttonwood Agreement signed by 24 brokers on 17 May 1792; constitution adopted 8 March 1817; 2006 merger with Archipelago formed the publicly traded NYSE Group; ICE acquired the NYSE in 2013; migration of its markets to NYSE Pillar marked in 2019. NYSE, History of NYSE: https://nyse.com/history-of-nyse
- 2025 records: NYSE equities ADV 3.35 bn shares (+40%), NYSE options ADV 10.5 M contracts, single-day closing auction record of over $205 bn. ICE, "ICE Announces 2025 Records...": https://s2.q4cdn.com/154085107/files/doc_news/ICE-Announces-2025-Records-Across-Its-Global-Derivative-and-NYSE-Equity--NYSE-Options-Markets-2026.pdf
- ICE FY2025 net revenue $9,931 M. Within it, cash equities and equity options $467 M, listings $495 M, and data and connectivity services $1,031 M (all ICE exchanges). Source: ICE Q4/FY2025 results, 8-K exhibit 99.1: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1571949/000110465926010646/tm264989d1_ex99-1.htm
- Floor, DMMs (one per listed stock, with an obligation to keep a fair and orderly market), floor brokers, and opening and closing auctions. "The only US equities exchange that operates a trading floor." https://www.nyse.com/trade/equities
- Integrated Feed: one sequenced stream of depth of book, trades, imbalances and status for NYSE, American, Arca, Texas and National. 2-byte size and 2-byte type header; types 100 Add, 102 Delete, 103 Execution, 105 Imbalance (once a second during auctions). https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/data/NYSE_Pillar_Integrated_Feed_Client_Specification.pdf
