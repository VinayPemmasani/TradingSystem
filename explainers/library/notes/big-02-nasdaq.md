# big-02-nasdaq: Nasdaq

## Key facts and sources
- Launched 8 Feb 1971 by NASD as an automated quotation system. Market makers updated quotes electronically, and "Nasdaq, of course, has never had a physical floor." IPO in 2005. Source: Nasdaq, "50 years of market innovation" (2021): https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/nasdaq%3A-50-years-of-market-innovation-2021-02-11
- From the 10-K for FY2025:
  - Founded in 1971 as a subsidiary of the regulator now called FINRA, and an independent exchange from 2007.
  - Combined with OMX AB in Feb 2008; bought Adenza (AxiomSL, Calypso) in 2023.
  - Powers more than 135 marketplaces, 19 of them its own; TotalView is its flagship depth product.
  - Source: https://ir.nasdaq.com/static-files/8f3e2b75-fce8-460b-8c9f-9ced29db8c42
- FY2025 results (https://ir.nasdaq.com/node/109901):
  - Net revenue $5,249 M, of which solutions $4,011 M.
  - Market Services $1,201 M.
  - Capital Access Platforms $2,137 M, with index $827 M.
  - Financial Technology $1,850 M.
  - Index ETP AUM $882 bn at year end.
  - Matched market share: US cash equities 14.2%, US equity options 29.1% (six markets), Nordic and Baltic 72.2%.
  - 4,480 companies listed on The Nasdaq Stock Market and 281 IPOs.
- ITCH Add Order is 36 bytes (field layout from /home/claude/ts-push/docs/specs/itch50-layout.md). The lab's day of 30 Jan 2019 has 368,366,634 messages (from the ac-01 note and the quant-real log).

## Unsure / left out

- "About three quarters" means solutions revenue divided by net revenue, which is 76%.
- The Market Services figure includes some European and US trading services; I call it "trading" as a simplification.
