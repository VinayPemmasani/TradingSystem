# big-03-cme: CME Group

## Key facts and sources
- Milestones, from the CME Group history page (https://www.cmegroup.com/ko/about/history-of-cme-group.html, Korean edition):
  - 1848: world's first futures exchange, in Chicago.
  - 1865: futures clearing with margin deposits.
  - 1972: first financial futures, on seven currencies.
  - 1992: Globex launches.
  - 1997: E-mini S&P 500.
  - 2007: CBOT merger.
  - 2008: NYMEX acquisition.
- 2025 ADV: a record 28.1 M contracts, of which:
  - interest rates 14.2 M (Treasury 8.3 M, SOFR 5.4 M)
  - equity index 7.4 M
  - energy 2.7 M
  - agricultural 1.9 M
  - metals 0.99 M, FX 0.98 M, crypto 0.28 M
  - Source: CME release, 5 Jan 2026, https://investor.cmegroup.com/node/54821
- 2025 revenue: $6,520.6 M in total; clearing and transaction fees $5,281.1 M; market data $803.1 M. Source: CME release, 4 Feb 2026, https://www.cmegroup.com/media-room/press-releases/2026/2/04/cme_group_inc_reportsfourthconsecutiveyearofrecordannualrevenuea.html
- FIA 2025 exchange ranking: CME Group 7,063,891,027 contracts, no. 4 after NSE, BSE and B3, and the largest US group. https://www.fia.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/Q4%202025%20ETD%20webinar.pdf
- CME Group held 60.8% of global interest rate options volume in 2025: WFE FY 2025 Market Highlights, https://www.world-exchanges.org/our-work/articles/fy-2025-market-highlights
- Globex supports FIFO and pro rata style algorithms per product: CME client systems wiki, https://cmegroupclientsite.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EPICSANDBOX/pages/457316046/CME+Globex+Matching+Algorithms

## Unsure / left out
- The "1848" beat is CBOT heritage. The CME page says only "first futures exchange established in Chicago".
- "Positions opened at CME close at CME" is the standard property of exchange-specific futures cleared by the exchange's own CCP. I did not find a single primary quote for it.
- No open-interest figure is used, because the year-end OI was not in the releases I read.
- iLink is named without a version.
