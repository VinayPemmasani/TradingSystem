# big-07-eurex: Deutsche Börse and Eurex

## Key facts and sources
- Timeline: 1585 Frankfurt exchange-rate fixing; DTB opened in January 1990; Xetra on 28 Nov 1997; Eurex in June 1998, the first cross-border futures exchange (DTB plus SOFFEX); IPO on 5 Feb 2001; full ownership of Clearstream in 2002. Proposed mergers were prohibited in 2012 (NYSE Euronext) and 2017 (LSEG). Source: https://www.deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/about-us/deutsche-boerse-group/company-history
- Eurex 2025 volumes: 2,065.9 M contracts (−1%), split into interest rate 1,045.6 M, index 699.7 M and equity 309.0 M. Eurex Clearing's OTC notional outstanding was €43,668 bn, of which IRS €19,991 bn. Source: https://www.deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/media/news-stories/press-releases/Full-Year-and-December-2025-Figures-at-Eurex-4891490
- FY2025 net revenue was €6,026 M. Source: https://deutsche-boerse.com/resource/blob/4887536/a68752b1b7c85f9cf530531c0c221dc4/data/company-release-q4-2025-en.pdf
  - Trading & Clearing: €2,553 M in total, including financial derivatives €1,193 M, commodities €612 M, cash equities €343 M and FX & digital €182 M.
  - Securities Services: €1,605 M.
  - Investment Management Solutions: €1,331 M.
  - Fund Services: €537 M.
- T7 technology (source: https://www.cashmarket.deutsche-boerse.com/resource/blob/4209806/cb4d5490d959bdac7acb5e604464a1f9/data/T7_Release_13.1_-_Function_And_Interface_Overview_Version_1.pdf):
  - One technical instance can host Eurex, Xetra and EEX.
  - EOBI is the most granular feed.
  - EMDI/MDI aggregate by price level.
  - ETI offers the highest throughput and lowest latency; FIX LF uses FIX 4.4.
