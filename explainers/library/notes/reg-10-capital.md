# reg-10-capital: Margin and capital for trading firms

## Key facts and sources
- **Reg T:** 12 CFR 220.12(a): margin equity security 50% of current market value. https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/220.12
- **FINRA 4210(c)(1):** maintenance 25% of current market value of long margin securities. https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4210
- **SEC Rule 15c3-1:** $250,000 minimum if carrying customer accounts (a)(2)(i); $100,000 for dealers (a)(2)(iii); haircut 15% of the greater of long or short for most equities, plus 15% of the lesser side's excess over 25% of the greater (c)(2)(vi)(J). https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/240.15c3-1
- **EU:** IFR/IFD apply from Jun 26, 2021; requirement is the highest of PMR, FOR (1/4 annual fixed overheads) and K-factors (Class 2); PMR EUR 75k/150k/750k. https://finance.ec.europa.eu/capital-markets-union-and-financial-markets/financial-markets/prudential-rules-investment-firms_en ; IFD Art. 9(1) EUR 750,000 initial capital for dealing on own account.
- **Worked numbers:** $100k with $50k loan: call below V = 66,667. Lab: max_position 2,000 x $50 = $100,000; Reg T $50,000; 15% haircut $15,000 (illustrative).
