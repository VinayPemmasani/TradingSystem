# ac-09-rates: Interest rate swaps

Key facts and sources
- OTC interest rate derivatives turnover $7.9 tn/day in April 2025 (EUR $3.0 tn, USD $2.4 tn): BIS press release, 30 Sep 2025, https://www.bis.org/press/p250930.htm
- OTC derivatives notional $846 tn at end-June 2025, 79% of it interest rate derivatives: BIS, https://www.bis.org/publ/otc_hy2512.htm
- Required transactions must execute on a SEF via an order book or an RFQ system (with an order book) sent to no fewer than three participants: 17 CFR 37.9, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/37.9
- Swaps reported to an SDR as soon as technologically practicable, SDR disseminates publicly (block delays under 43.5): 17 CFR 43.3, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/43.3
- Cleared swaps are marked once daily at CME Clearing: CME Financial Safeguards, https://www.cmegroup.com/clearing/files/financialsafeguards.pdf
- DV01: computed, $100 m × annuity factor 8.19 (10 annual periods at 3.8%) × 1 bp ≈ $82,000.

Unsure / left out
- No lab tie-in: the repo has no rates or swaps code, so the video has six scenes.
- The 3.80% rate and floating payments are illustrative; the annuity ignores discounting off a real curve.
- LCH and CME are named as examples of swap CCPs; no clearing market shares given (not verified).
- "Made available to trade" scope is simplified; the trade execution requirement applies to swaps subject to the clearing mandate and made available to trade.
