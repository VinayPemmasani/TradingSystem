Can a market data engineer's stack, plus MiroFish news sentiment, run a fund that makes money?

You built a Nasdaq feed, order books, an exchange and a firm, then asked whether the strategies on top of it, mixed with a sentiment swarm, could turn $5bn of paper money into profit. This page reruns every model from the project, scores them all with the same tests, and animates the result.

What is being simulated

One machine, three test benches

Every strategy runs through the same chain. Market data comes in, a book is built, signals are formed, risk checks the order, the order is filled and PnL is booked. The three benches differ in what feeds the chain and what is assumed.

How to analyse a result

Eight questions, in the order a risk committee asks them

A backtest that makes money has only passed the first question. Each question below says what it asks, how to compute it, and what this project's numbers say. All of them are now one command: python3 -m paperfund master.

The full scorecard, Bench A

Every sleeve, every test

Sharpe is annualised. The 95% range comes from 2,000 block-bootstrap resamples. P(SR>0) is the probabilistic Sharpe ratio. Deflated is the same probability after allowing for every variant tried. Beta and alpha are against QQQ.
